How to use this page. Every frame is kept unless you exclude it. In each clip, press exclude from on the first frame of a stretch you do not want, then to here on its last frame; a clip can have several excluded ranges, also in the middle. Every and offset thin a clip on its original frame order before exclusions, so excluding frames never shifts which frames are sampled. Click a picture to enlarge it (arrow keys move, Esc closes). You can download the selection at any time; nothing on disk changes here. The page does not check that frames overlap: a changed selection needs its own camera solve and camera review.
